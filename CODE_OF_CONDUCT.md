# Code of Conduct

Moon Technologies projects are built by people who are learning, teaching and creating together.
We want every community space — issues, pull requests, discussions and chats — to feel like moonlight:
calm, warm and safe for everyone.

## Our standards

**Do:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, explain the *why* behind feedback, give credit, accept
corrections gracefully, and help newcomers.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexualised, discriminatory or violent remarks; publish other
people's private information; or disrupt conversations on purpose.

These standards apply regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual orientation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, suspend or ban participants who break this code.
Report problems privately through GitHub's **Report content** option or by contacting the organization owners.
All reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
